# ISB PGP YL Application Essays - Refined Drafts

## Essay 1: Leadership Experience
**Topic:** Describe a specific personal situation in which you assumed a leadership role without formal authority. How did you motivate and influence others during this experience? What challenges did you encounter, and what insights did you gain about your leadership abilities as a result of this experience?

### Refined Draft
During my tenure in school management, the institution launched an ambitious project to create custom workbooks for grades 9 and 10, aiming to provide a cost-effective, high-quality alternative to expensive market publications. Initially, I was assigned to design the cover and illustrations, leveraging my **Industrial Design background and proficiency with tools like Figma and SolidWorks**. However, when the scriptwriter unexpectedly left a month before the academic session, the project stalled. Recognizing the urgency, I stepped up to lead the initiative without formal authority.

To bridge the gap, I formed a cross-functional team of 10 faculty members. The subject teachers drafted content, while the computer faculty handled formatting. Motivating the team was challenging; many were hesitant due to time constraints and unfamiliarity with collaborative tools like Google Docs. To address this, I conducted brief onboarding sessions and centralized drafts in shared folders for real-time review. Drawing on my design experience, I created formatted templates with predefined styles for headings and body text to ensure uniformity, significantly reducing the formatting burden on the team.

Despite the tight deadline, we completed the 300-page workbook on time. Ideally, this resource improved classroom outcomes and allowed for better assignment tracking. For the students, it was a financial relief, and for the faculty, it was a technical upskilling opportunity. This experience reinforced my belief that leadership is about empowering others through systems and empathy. It taught me that even without a title, one can lead by streamlining processes and fostering a collaborative culture—insights I am eager to bring to group projects at ISB.

## Essay 2: Career Goals
**Topic:** What impact do you aim to create after completing the PGP YL programme? Please share your medium- to long-term career plans, including any specific challenges you anticipate facing along the way?

### Refined Draft
Growing up, I witnessed my parents transform a small idea into a thriving education business. This journey inspired me to pursue a path where I could create impact beyond individual success. My background in **Industrial Design from NIT Rourkela** instilled in me a problem-solving mindset, which I further honed as an Assistant Manager in my family’s firm. Whether analyzing fee-payment patterns or managing daily operations, I realized my passion aligns with the strategic problem-solving core to consulting.

Recently, my experience at **EmLyon Business School**, particularly leading the "BlendFast" project to validate a market gap for healthy workplace food options, solidified my interest in business strategy. However, to transition from an operational role to a strategic consultant, I need a structured understanding of corporate dynamics. This is why I am pursuing the ISB PGP YL. The program’s comprehensive curriculum and experiential learning opportunities are the perfect bridge to my goals.

Post-PGP YL, I aspire to join a top-tier consulting firm, where I can apply **design thinking** to solve complex business challenges. In the long run, I plan to establish a consulting firm focused on empowering social-impact businesses to achieve sustainable growth—mirroring the impact I’ve seen in the education sector. I anticipate challenges, such as adapting to the high-pressure environment of global consulting and navigating competitive markets. However, I view these as opportunities to refine my resilience and adaptability, traits I have cultivated through my diverse experiences in design, education management, and international projects.

## Essay 3: Unique Contribution
**Topic:** What unique experiences and perspectives do you bring to your class? How do you intend to contribute to the diversity of thought and ideas within the programme? Please provide specific examples to illustrate your points?

### Refined Draft
My journey from an engineering background in Design to managing a school in a Tier-2 city (Unnao) has shaped my unique perspective on consumer behavior and socio-economic dynamics. Studying at a premier institute like NIT Rourkela surrounded by India's brightest minds, and later working with families from diverse economic backgrounds, taught me how environment shapes values.

For instance, during my time in school management, I used **data analysis tools (Python/Excel)** to study fee-payment patterns. I observed a counterintuitive trend: lower-income families with daily wage earners were often more consistent with payments than middle-class families with steady incomes. Further analysis revealed that payment defaults were often linked to a lack of engagement with the school. I hypothesized that strengthening the parent-teacher bond could solve this. By implementing targeted interventions—mentoring sessions and structured interactions—we improved relationships and **reduced payment defaults by ~15% (300k INR)**.

This ability to derive data-backed insights from human behavior is what I bring to the ISB cohort. My experience is not just theoretical; it is grounded in the grassroots reality of managing an education business and executing projects like waste management drives as a volunteer. At ISB, I intend to contribute this blend of **analytical rigor and social empathy** to classroom discussions. Whether debating business ethics or analyzing case studies, I will offer a perspective that balances business objectives with ground-level social realities, enriching the peer learning experience.
