Olist Brazilian Marketplace Case Study

● 100,000+ Transactions ● 9 Datasets Merged ● Python · SQL · Tableau ● 5 Interactive Dashboards

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

In the highly competitive e-commerce landscape, customer acquisition costs (CAC) are rising. For a marketplace like Olist, the primary challenge is not just finding new customers, but maximizing the Lifetime Value (CLV) of existing ones.

The Problem: Raw transactional data is "silent." Without a structured analytical framework, the business cannot distinguish between a one-time buyer and a loyal brand advocate. This leads to inefficient marketing spend, where "At-Risk" customers are ignored and "VIPs" are under-served. We need to transform 100,000 rows of fragmented data into a clear Retention Strategy.

2. Methodology & Data Dictionary

To solve this, we architected a relational data environment by merging 9 separate datasets. Below are the primary tables engineered for this analysis:

  • A. The Recency Table (Temporal Engine): Snapshot Date (Max Date + 5 days) used to calculate Days_since_last_order.
  • B. The VIP Table (Premium Customer Tracker): Aggregated orders merged with financial core to track CLV.
  • C. The Monetary Table (Financial Core): Tracks total revenue contribution and geographic wealth distribution across Brazil.
  • D. The Product Affinity Table (Segment Context): Maps high-value segments to specific product categories for targeted marketing.

Step 1: Building the Recency Table

Establishing the temporal baseline involves merging customer base with orders. We drop all non-delivered items to avoid skewing metrics. Snapshot Date (Max Date + 5 days) allows for a stable measurement of relationship "freshness."

import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

# Merging orders and customers
merged = pd.merge(orders, customers, on='customer_id', how='left')
merged = merged[merged['order_status'] == 'delivered'].copy()

# Establishing Snapshot Date
merged['order_purchase_timestamp'] = pd.to_datetime(merged['order_purchase_timestamp'])
snapshot_date = merged['order_purchase_timestamp'].max() + pd.Timedelta(days=5)

# Calculating Recency Core Value
merged['Days_since_last_order'] = (snapshot_date - merged['order_purchase_timestamp']).dt.days

6. Next Steps & Recovery Plan

Identifying the "Economic Heart" and the "Staircase to Churn" allows for surgical precision in marketing. The next phase involves using the Product Affinity engine to automate "Next Best Offer" campaigns for customers in the 60-90 day churn window.
